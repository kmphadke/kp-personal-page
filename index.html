About Me

Bringing academic rigor and three decades of operational depth to AI product building

With more than 30 years in consulting and academia, I prepare future professionals to excel in technology-driven environments. I have mentored over 1,000 students and industry participants, integrating practical insights with rigorous academic frameworks to clarify and operationalize complex ideas.

Currently, I am developing EduAssess, an AI-powered platform that enables universities to conduct multi-round mock interviews, generate intelligent assessments, and prepare students for placements with comprehensive analytics. EduAssess incorporates advanced concepts from the 24-week AI Catalyst Program—agentic systems, multi-agent workflows, and AI-driven product development—applied to a domain I know intimately. With 23 years of industry experience and 13 years in academia teaching and mentoring over 1,000 students, EduAssess represents the fusion of operational expertise and modern AI tools.

As an independent consultant and visiting faculty at a leading management institute in Pune, I have designed and taught courses in Economics, Statistics, Time Series Analysis and Business Simulation. By translating complex concepts into relatable examples and practical exercises, I enable learners to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world challenges.

At State Street Global Advisors, I led cross-departmental initiatives that increased operational efficiency by 30% and streamlined workflows across various teams. As Senior Developer and Data Architect at Harvard University, I designed and implemented more than 400 table structures for the Grants Management Application Suite.

Beyond the classroom, I mentor students through capstone projects and community education initiatives, empowering them to build confidence and critical-thinking skills essential for success.

AI-powered platform for student placement preparation and intelligent assessments.

A purpose-built platform for higher education, assisting universities in preparing students for placements, conducting intelligent assessments, and mapping academic outcomes. EduAssess combines advanced AI capabilities with a thorough understanding of the Indian higher education landscape.
